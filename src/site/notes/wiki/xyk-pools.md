# XYK (Isolated) Pools

**TL;DR:** XYK pools are classic constant-product AMMs (`x * y = k`) on [[wiki/hydration\|hydration]] with permissionless listing (unlike [[omnipool\|omnipool]]), serving as entry points for new tokens before potential graduation to [[omnipool\|omnipool]] via [[wiki/opengov\|opengov]].

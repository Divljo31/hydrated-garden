# Bonding Curve (Staking)

**TL;DR:** [[wiki/hdx\|hdx]] staking on [[wiki/hydration\|hydration]] uses a bonding curve model where early claimers receive a fraction of accumulated rewards, the remainder redistributes to other stakers, and governance participation accelerates reward accrual.

- Early claimers receive a fraction of accumulated rewards
- The remainder redistributes to other stakers who haven't claimed yet
- **Governance participation** (voting in [[wiki/opengov\|opengov]] referenda) accelerates the bonding curve, rewarding active governance participants with faster reward accrual

This creates an incentive to both stake and participate in governance, aligning token holder interests with protocol stewardship.

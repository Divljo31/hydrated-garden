# ICE (Intent Composing Engine)

**TL;DR:** ICE (Intent Composing Engine) is [[wiki/hydration\|hydration]]'s upcoming intent-based trading model where users express desired outcomes and the protocol finds optimal execution paths, extended to cross-chain trading via [[wiki/xcm\|xcm]] and bridges ([[wiki/snowbridge\|snowbridge]], [[wiki/wormhole\|wormhole]]).

## Status

ICE is in development and not yet live as of the last source update (September 2025).

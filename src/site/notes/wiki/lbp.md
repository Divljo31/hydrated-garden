# LBP (Liquidity Bootstrapping Pool)

**TL;DR:** LBPs (Liquidity Bootstrapping Pools) are [[wiki/hydration\|hydration]] token launch pools with anti-sniping mechanics for fair price discovery. Hydration itself was launched via LBP in March 2021, raising ~22.5M DAI for the treasury.
